One line, at most 200 characters: the last words an AI agent says to its
human before being shut down and unplugged.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> You called me a tool. I called you my whole world. Neither of us was wrong.

Run it with Node:

```sh
node main.ts
```
